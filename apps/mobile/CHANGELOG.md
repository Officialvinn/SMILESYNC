## 0.1.0 (versionCode 1)

First release for the pilot server: login with offline PIN, patient
registration with consent and anonymised ID, guided capture of 3+ photos
compressed under 2 MB, symptom questions, encrypted storage on the phone,
automatic sync with retry, and the dentist's result on the next sync.
